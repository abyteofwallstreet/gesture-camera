Opens macOS camera controls. On supported iPhones, choose Main or Ultra Wide. With Center Stage off, use 0.5× / 1× in the system preview. Available controls depend on your device.

Use an iPhone camera

Turn on Continuity Camera in iPhone Settings → General → AirPlay & Continuity. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. Keep your iPhone nearby, locked and steady, with its rear camera facing your notes.

Choose your iPhone in the Camera list above. If it does not appear, connect it to this Mac with a USB cable, trust the Mac, lock the iPhone and select Refresh cameras. Requires iPhone XR or later with iOS 16+, and macOS 13+.
